# Mehta Dental Clinic — FAQs

Placeholder FAQ content for development. Replace with the real pilot clinic's
answers before going live (see docs/BUILD_PLAN.md Phase 10).

## Cleaning ki fees kitni hai?
Cleaning (scaling and polishing) ka charge ₹500 hai.

## Consultation ka charge kya hai?
Pehli consultation ₹200 mein hoti hai. Agar treatment start karte hain, yeh fee adjust ho jaati hai.

## Clinic kitne baje khulta hai?
Clinic subah 10 baje se raat 8 baje tak khula rehta hai, Sunday band rehta hai.

## Lunch break kab hota hai?
Dopahar 2 se 3 baje tak doctor lunch break par hote hain, is waqt appointment nahi milegi.

## Clinic Sunday ko khula rehta hai kya?
Nahi, clinic har Sunday band rehta hai.

## Parking ki facility hai kya?
Haan, clinic ke saamne free parking available hai, do-pehiya aur char-pehiya dono ke liye.

## Payment kaise kar sakte hain?
Cash, UPI, debit/credit card aur Paytm — sab accepted hain.

## Health insurance accept karte hain kya?
Kuch cashless insurance tie-ups hain. Apna insurance card saath laayein, staff verify kar degi.

## Clinic kahan located hai?
Clinic Sukhadia Circle, Udaipur mein hai, 12 number par.

## Emergency mein kya karein?
Dant mein severe dard ya emergency ho to clinic ke emergency number par call karein, woh number alag se share kiya jaata hai.

## Bachon ka treatment hota hai kya?
Haan, hum bachon ke liye pediatric dental care bhi karte hain, 3 saal se upar ke bachon ke liye.

## Root canal mein kitna time lagta hai?
Root canal normally ek ghante ka session hota hai, kabhi kabhi 2 sessions lagte hain.

## Teeth whitening ka charge kya hai?
Teeth whitening ka charge ₹3000 hai, ek hi session mein ho jaata hai.

## Appointment cancel kar sakte hain kya?
Haan, aap call karke ya WhatsApp par appointment cancel ya reschedule kar sakte hain.

## Doctor kaun hain?
Clinic mein BDS/MDS qualified dentist practice karte hain.

## Second opinion ke liye aa sakte hain kya?
Haan, second opinion consultations bhi li jaati hain, normal consultation fee applicable hogi.

## X-ray ki facility hai kya?
Haan, clinic mein hi digital X-ray ki facility available hai.

## Filling karane mein kitna time lagta hai?
Normal filling 45 minute ke andar ho jaati hai.
